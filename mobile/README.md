# ScreenFly Mobile Components
*See Yourself From New Perspectives - Mobile Edition*

This directory contains the mobile application components for ScreenFly by Coevolvenetwork, bringing the fly-on-the-wall perspective to your pocket-sized digital world.

## Overview

The ScreenFly mobile version provides screen monitoring capabilities for iOS and Android devices with privacy-first design principles. Like a street photographer capturing candid moments in the urban landscape, ScreenFly Mobile documents your authentic mobile interactions while respecting your digital privacy.

## Mobile Fly-on-the-Wall Philosophy

ScreenFly Mobile transforms your phone into an unobtrusive observer of your digital habits. It quietly captures the raw, unfiltered essence of how you truly interact with your mobile device, revealing patterns and behaviors that traditional analytics miss.
