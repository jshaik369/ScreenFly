# ScreenFly by Coevolvenetwork
*See Yourself From New Perspectives. A project by Coevolvenetwork.*

An open-source cross-platform privacy-first screen monitoring solution that provides comprehensive screen watching capabilities across desktop, mobile, browser, and embedded systems. Like a fly on the wall of the digital world, ScreenFly captures the authentic moments and perspectives that matter most.

## About

ScreenFly by Coevolvenetwork believes that technology should serve people, not the other way around. Born from the vision that monitoring solutions should be transparent, privacy-respecting, and truly collaborative, ScreenFly embodies the spirit of street photography in the digital realm—capturing genuine, unposed moments while respecting the subjects.

Like a photographer who sees the world through fresh eyes, ScreenFly helps you discover new perspectives on your digital interactions. We're building more than just software – we're fostering a community where diverse perspectives strengthen our collective capability to create meaningful, ethical technology.

Our commitment to privacy-first design ensures that users maintain complete control over their data while benefiting from powerful monitoring capabilities across all their devices and platforms. Every moment captured is yours to control, yours to understand.

## Features

- **Cross-Platform Compatibility**: Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux), Mobile (iOS, Android), Browser extensions, and Embedded systems
- **Privacy-First Architecture**: Local-first processing, transparent data handling, and configurable privacy settings
- **Open-Source Transparency**: Fully auditable codebase with community-driven development
- **Modular Design**: Component-based architecture allowing selective deployment
- **Extensible Plugin System**: Support for custom integrations and third-party extensions
- **Fly-on-the-Wall Perspective**: Unobtrusive monitoring that captures authentic digital interactions
- **Street Photography Inspired**: Clean, honest documentation of digital moments without artificial staging

## Privacy-First Approach

ScreenFly is built on these core principles, reflecting our fly-on-the-wall philosophy:

- **User Data Sovereignty**: You own and control your data completely
- **Transparent Processing**: All data handling is visible and auditable
- **Configurable Privacy**: Granular controls over what data is collected and how
- **Local-First Design**: Processing happens on your devices when possible
- **Open-Source Auditability**: Every line of code is available for inspection
- **Respectful Observation**: Like ethical street photography, we observe without intrusion
